> [!mechanic] Marionette strings
> **Marionette strings** are the primary resource for some of your abilities.
>
> > [!example]+ Using strings
> > You (Level 2 character) are in a battle. You choose to use **Follow your heart!** on the enemy.
> >
> > Next round, you use **String along** to move them away from yourself.
>
> The used strings can be attacked.
>
> > [!question]+ How does **marionette** work with instant abilities?
> > It's possible to cut strings even when used with these kinds of abilities. For example, when using **String along**, someone can try to attack.
